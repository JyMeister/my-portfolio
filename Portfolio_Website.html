About Me

Hi, I'm Jay Braun, a junior web developer who enjoys building websites that are clean, functional, and a little creative. I started teaching myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WordPress while working full-time, and over time it turned into something I really enjoy doing.

Before coding, I worked in various jobs learning a multitude of new skills in automotive body painting, law enforcement, ammunition manufacturing and assembly lines, jobs that taught me how to stay focused, adapt quickly, multitask and pay attention to detail. Now, I use those same skills when I'm working on websites, managing projects, figuring out issues, or furthering my education.

I'm currently doing freelance work on sites with blogs, e-commerce, and media integration, and I'm always trying to learn something new to improve my builds. Outside of coding, I'm a big fan of gaming, especially titles like Baldur's Gate 3, Minecraft, and Dead by Daylight. I also love various table top games including Magic: the Gathering, and Dungeons & Dragons. I often pull inspiration from those worlds into my designs.

Whether I'm writing code or playing games, I enjoy solving problems, being creative, and finding new ways to grow.
